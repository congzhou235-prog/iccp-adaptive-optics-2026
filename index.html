Also prepare a compatible USB 3.0 camera cable, an SM1 spanner wrench, clean gloves, and a computer with the camera driver and Galaxy Viewer. A collimated beam is needed to check focus and alignment.

Put it together.
Bring the spots into focus.

The existing guide includes photos for each stage, camera setup, and an example of the final spot pattern.

Read the illustrated guide ↗

  1. Insert the nuts during printing

    Pause the print when the recessed holes are accessible. Press the four M2×2 brass insert nuts into the matching recesses, then resume printing.

  2. Fit the camera

    Place the board in the front housing. Fit the back housing and tighten the four M2 screws gently into the embedded nuts.

  3. Mount the array

    Hold the microlens array between two retaining rings in the lens tube, then attach the tube to the housing.

  4. Start the camera

    Connect the camera and open the live image in Galaxy Viewer. Center the sensor in the collimated beam.

  5. Focus and align

    Adjust the array position until the spots are small and clear. Rotate the array to align the grid, then secure it gently.
