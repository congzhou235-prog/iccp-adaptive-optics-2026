The camera housing.

PART 01

Front housing

Holds the camera board and provides the SM1 opening for the lens tube. The sensor faces this opening.

PART 02

Back housing

Secures the camera board with four screws. The opening lines up with the camera connector.

STL files, editable CAD files, print material, settings, and screw sizes are not available yet. These details are needed to reproduce the housing.

03 / ASSEMBLY

Put it together.
Bring the spots into focus.

The existing guide includes photos for each stage, camera setup, and an example of the final spot pattern.

Read the illustrated guide ↗

  1. Fit the camera

    Place the board in the front housing. Fit the back housing and tighten the four screws gently.

  2. Mount the array

    Hold the microlens array between two retaining rings in the lens tube, then attach the tube to the housing.

  3. Start the camera

    Connect the camera and open the live image in Galaxy Viewer. Center the sensor in the collimated beam.

  4. Focus and align

    Adjust the array position until the spots are small and clear. Rotate the array to align the grid, then secure it gently.
